Privacy Policy

How Speed Assignment collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our platform.

Last Updated: September 29, 2026 Speed Assignment Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Speed Assignment. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information provided by users of our platform in a responsible and transparent manner. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, submit forms, or communicate with us, how that information is used, and the steps taken to safeguard it.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly provided by users when interacting with our platform, including:

  • Contact details such as full name, email address, and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Task details, service requirements, instructions, and messages submitted via forms.
  • Reference materials or files submitted in relation to service inquiries.
  • Communication history when contacting our support team via email or messaging platforms.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through Speed Assignment is used strictly for legitimate operational purposes, such as:

  • Responding to contact inquiries and assessing academic or technical project requirements.
  • Providing assistance, quotes, and communication regarding requested services.
  • Managing website administration and technical functionality.
  • Improving user experience and service responsiveness.

4. Contact Form and Communication Data

When you submit inquiries through our online contact form, the information you provide (such as your name, email, phone number, and service requirements) is processed to fulfill your request and maintain communication. We do not sell, rent, or trade contact form submissions to external marketing agencies.

5. Uploaded Documents and Files

Any documents, project guidelines, image uploads, or reference materials shared with Speed Assignment are used solely for reviewing and completing the requested services. Files are accessed only by personnel authorized to handle your inquiry.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our platform uses standard web technologies to maintain basic website performance, navigational structure, and user interface preferences. Standard browser session storage or local storage may be used to store temporary display states or preferences. You can adjust your browser settings to refuse or restrict cookies if desired.

7. Third-Party Services

Speed Assignment may utilize standard third-party tools (such as map displays, messaging shortcuts, or external font/icon distribution networks) to deliver standard website features. These third-party providers operate independently under their respective terms and privacy practices.

8. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect submitted information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to user data is limited to authorized administrators responsible for operating the service.

9. Data Retention

Communication records, contact form requests, and uploaded project specifications are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which they were collected or to fulfill ongoing service inquiries.

10. User Rights and Choices

You have the right to request access to the personal information you have submitted to us, ask for corrections, or request deletion of your contact records. To make a request regarding your data, please reach out to us using the contact details provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

Speed Assignment provides academic and professional assistance services intended for university students and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures or service functionality. Updated versions will be posted directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Information
